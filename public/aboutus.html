About PathPilot

PathPilot is an AI-powered career development platform built by 5 second-semester students as their Capstone-1 project. PathPilot simplifies career growth by providing personalized AI coaching, resume building, cover letter generation, mock interview preparation, and industry insights. Our platform helps users navigate their career journey with confidence, leveraging advanced AI models to deliver tailored guidance that makes sense for their individual profiles, skills, and goals.
